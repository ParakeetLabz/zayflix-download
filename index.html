How to install it on your TV

  1. Get the .apk onto your TV — USB drive, Downloads, or wherever your TV's file picker shows it.
  2. Open the file. The first time, you may need to allow ZayFlix to install unknown apps in your TV settings.
  3. Do NOT uninstall the old ZayFlix first — v13 updates right over v9/v10/v11/v12, same package and signer, so your My Apps picks carry over.
  4. Press Home — pick ZayFlix and choose Always / Set as default. Press Home again to check it goes straight to ZayFlix OS.
  5. Don't disable Google TV yet. Only do that after ZayFlix is working as Home, or you'll have no home screen at all.
Try ZayFlix in a browser instead (HTML version)

The browser version can't launch apps installed on a TV or phone — tapping a tile opens that app's website instead. For the real launcher, use the .apk above.
